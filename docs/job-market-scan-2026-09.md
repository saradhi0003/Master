## 4. Strategic read

- **Differentiator:** most Agentforce resumes are declarative-only admins or pure advisory architects. A hands-on builder (Apex + LWC + Data 360 + Agentforce + security models) who also leads the program at a named marquee client is top-decile for FDE, Technical Builder and Architect roles.
- **Gaps to close before interviews:** Python (several live JDs list it; even light scripting counts), and a RAG / Data Library story backed by one or two metrics.
- **Watch Salesforce careers weekly for FDE openings.** FDE is the closest in-house equivalent to a hands-on client build role, and Salesforce is staffing it aggressively. The hourly feed now checks Salesforce's Workday site for you.
- **Sources for the hourly feed:** Greenhouse / Lever boards of Salesforce partners (NeuraFlash, SII, Infosys / Accenture Salesforce practices, Slalom, Deloitte Digital, Capgemini), BuiltIn Austin, Dice saved searches.
